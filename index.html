FAQ

Common questions

What kind of businesses do you work with?

I work with early-stage startups that need to ship an AI-powered MVP fast, and small-to-medium businesses that need custom software — dashboards, automations, CRMs, internal tools — without the overhead of a full agency. If you're a team of 1–50 people with a real problem and a budget to solve it, we're probably a good fit.

Can you replace our Excel spreadsheets with a real web app?

Yes — this is one of the most common projects I take on. I take the logic your team has built in Excel or Google Sheets and turn it into a proper web application with real UI, user permissions, data validation, and no broken formulas. Most businesses are surprised how much faster their team moves once the data actually lives in a real system.

What's the difference between you and a dev agency?

You work directly with me — a senior engineer with 5 years of production experience — on every aspect of the project. No account managers, no handoffs to junior devs overseas, no markups. That means faster communication, better decisions, and a codebase you can actually maintain after I'm done.

Can you build an AI chatbot for my business?

Yes. I build AI chat assistants and knowledge tools that are trained on your actual business data — your docs, SOPs, product catalog, support history, whatever's relevant. These are production-grade RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) systems, not generic ChatGPT wrappers. They actually know your business.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope — but I work on both fixed-price projects (best for well-defined work) and hourly/retainer arrangements (best for ongoing or exploratory work). The free discovery call is the best way to get a real number. I'm transparent about pricing and will tell you honestly if something is out of scope.

Book a free 30-minute call or send me a message below. We'll talk through what you're trying to build, what's blocking you, and whether I'm the right fit. No pitch, no commitment required on either side.
